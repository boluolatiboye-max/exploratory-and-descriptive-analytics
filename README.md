# 🎓 The GenAI Paradox: Balancing Academic Efficacy & Student Well-Being

An exploratory and descriptive analytics project evaluating the impact of Generative AI adoption on academic performance (GPA), student mental health, and institutional policy dynamics across higher education.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
As Generative AI tools proliferate across academic institutions, educators face a dual challenge: maximizing academic gains while mitigating potential cognitive dependency and student burnout. Analyzing **50,000 student records**, this project explores how weekly GenAI usage, institutional policies, and paid subscriptions correlate with GPA trajectories, burnout risks, and exam anxiety levels.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Sample Size:** 50,000 Students
* **Average Pre-Semester GPA:** 3.15 / 4.00
* **Average Post-Semester GPA:** 3.35 / 4.00 (+0.20 GPA uplift)
* **Average Weekly GenAI Usage:** 8.43 Hours/Week

---

## 🔍 Core Data Findings

### 1. Academic Performance Growth
* **GPA Improvement:** Across all academic levels (Freshman to Graduate), Post-Semester GPAs consistently outperformed Pre-Semester GPAs, averaging **3.35** compared to **3.15** prior.
* **Major Breakdown:** **STEM** majors reported both the highest average GenAI usage (**10.49 hrs/week**) and the highest average Post-Semester GPA (**3.36**), followed closely by Medical and Humanities fields.

### 2. Mental Health & Burnout Distribution
* **Burnout Risk:** **42.29% (21.14K)** of students fall into the **Medium Burnout Risk** category, while **24.97% (12.49K)** exhibit **High Burnout Risk**, indicating that high AI usage does not automatically guarantee low stress.
* **Institutional Policy Impact:** Students in institutions with a **Strict Ban** on AI reported significantly higher exam anxiety levels (**~4.89**) compared to environments where AI is **Allowed with Citation** or **Actively Encouraged** (~4.12).

### 3. Subscription & Tool Adoption Dynamics
* **Paid Subscriptions:** **57.69% (28.85K)** rely on free-tier tools, while **42.31% (21.15K)** invest in premium GenAI subscriptions.
* **Prompt Engineering Skills:** Advanced prompt engineering correlates strongly with higher tool diversity and more structured academic workflows (debugging, ideation, and research synthesis).

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Clear Institutional Policy Guidelines:** Transition away from strict, punitive AI bans toward clear citation frameworks to directly reduce student exam anxiety without lowering academic standards.
2. **Burnout Intervention:** Implement academic counseling and digital wellness workshops targeting the **67%+** of students displaying medium-to-high burnout risk.
3. **AI Literacy Training:** Expand formal prompt engineering workshops across Non-STEM disciplines (Arts & Humanities) to bridge the usage and performance gap.

## 🚀 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow for investigating the complex relationship between Generative AI, academic performance, and student well-being.

The analysis moves from:

Raw Data → Cleaning → EDA → SQL → Statistical Analysis → Power BI → Insights → Recommendations

Rather than evaluating GenAI purely as a productivity tool, the project highlights the trade-off between academic efficacy and student well-being, demonstrating how data can be used to support more informed decisions around AI adoption in higher education.
